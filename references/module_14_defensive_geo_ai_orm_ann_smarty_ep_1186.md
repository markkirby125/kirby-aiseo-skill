# Module 14: Defensive GEO & AI Reputation Management (ORM)

*Source: Ann Smarty & Edward Sturm podcast Episode 1,186. October 3, 2026.*

### **14.1 The AI Overview Negative Sentiment Threat**
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) has historically focused on achieving positive citation and inclusion. However, Google's AI Overviews now appear for approximately **90% of branded search queries** (according to industry estimates discussed in Ep. 1186) and are proactively surfacing reasons to *avoid* a company before the user even clicks through to the brand's website. 

When a user searches for a brand name, AI Overviews actively synthesize and display:
*   Negative customer reviews aggregated from third-party platforms.
*   Complaints sourced directly from Reddit and community forums.
*   Historical controversies and product flaws.

To survive this conversational shift, brands must deploy active, defensive AI Reputation Management (ORM).

### **14.2 Human-Led Customer Support to Starve AI Models**
*   **The LLM Automation Trap:** Many companies are rapidly replacing human support staff with LLM chatbots to reduce operational costs. This creates extreme customer friction, leading to a spike in angry, highly detailed rants posted to public forums and social media.
*   **The Mitigation Strategy:** Prioritize empathetic, human-led customer support. Resolving issues privately and quickly prevents negative sentiment from ever being indexed on the open web, effectively starving AI models of the negative ammunition they need to build hostile brand overviews.

### **14.3 Crisis Management Pre-Planning**
*   **Proactive Strategy:** Large companies must have a pre-planned crisis response strategy. Reactive, emotional, or poorly framed responses during a PR incident provide concentrated, high-virality text data that LLMs ingest and immortalize in their weights. Controlled, pre-planned crisis communication minimizes the extraction of negative entity associations.
