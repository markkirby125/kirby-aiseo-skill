## **Module 6: Google Discover & Algorithmic Feed Optimization**

Google Discover drives 5M+ daily click feeds via mobile OS integration, evaluated purely on audience engagement signals rather than backlink weight.

* **"One Emotion, One Promise" Headline Formula:**
  * Structure headlines around explicit human desires or frustration (70–95 characters max).
  * Empirical benchmark: Emotion-driven Discover headlines achieve **14% CTR vs. 2% for standard SEO titles (+600% lift)**.
* **Visual Attention Trigger:**
  * Custom emotional imagery featuring human faces achieves **+30% to +50% CTR lift** over generic stock photography.
  * Image dimensions: Minimum 1200×800px; verified high-resolution OpenGraph markup.
* **The "Sniper Approach" (Anti-Volume Rule):**
  * Publishing >10 low-engagement articles/day dilutes site-wide user interaction scores and suppresses Discover distribution. High-intent, hyper-focused curation beats volume.
