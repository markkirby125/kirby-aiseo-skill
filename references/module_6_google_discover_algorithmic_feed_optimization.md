# Module 6: Google Discover & Algorithmic Feed Optimization

Google Discover is a mobile feed surfaced through OS integration. It reportedly drives click volumes in the millions per day for large publishers and is reported to weight audience engagement signals over backlinks (practitioner-reported, single-source, unverified; treat as directional).

* **"One Emotion, One Promise" Headline Formula:**
  * Structure headlines around explicit human desires or frustration (70–95 characters max).
  * Reported benchmark: Emotion-driven Discover headlines achieve **14% CTR vs. 2% for standard SEO titles (+600% lift; single-source, unverified)**.
* **Visual Attention Trigger:**
  * Custom emotional imagery featuring human faces achieves **+30% to +50% CTR lift** (reported, single-source, unverified) over generic stock photography.
  * Image dimensions: Minimum 1200×800px; verified high-resolution OpenGraph markup.
* **The "Sniper Approach" (Anti-Volume Rule):**
  * Publishing >10 low-engagement articles/day reportedly dilutes site-wide user interaction scores and suppresses Discover distribution. High-intent, hyper-focused curation beats volume.
